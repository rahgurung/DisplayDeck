Which one is right for you?

They overlap a lot. The differences are in focus and price.

DisplayDeck

A small, free menu bar app for the everyday controls: real brightness, contrast, volume and input switching, plus resolution, refresh rate and mirroring. Monitors can follow your MacBook's brightness, and custom shortcuts work on any keyboard. Opens with no security warnings and updates itself.

MonitorControl

The long-standing free, open-source option, with one slider or shortcut for every display at once.

Lunar

The automation app: brightness from the ambient light sensor, sunrise and sunset, per-app presets, dimming below the minimum and extra XDR brightness. Many of these need Lunar Pro.

BetterDisplay

The power-user toolbox: custom and HiDPI resolutions, virtual displays, picture in picture and EDID overrides, with brightness control alongside. Advanced features need Pro.

DisplayBuddy

A polished paid app covering brightness, contrast, volume, inputs and presets, according to its own site.
